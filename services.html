How an Online Mathematics Lesson Works

  1. The student contacts me through Messenger, Telegram or SMS and explains what topic or problem they need help with.
  2. We choose a suitable lesson time and I add the lesson to my custom scheduling and accounting system.
  3. At the agreed time, we connect online through Jitsi Meet.
  4. During the lesson, I use a graphics tablet and digital whiteboard to explain theory, write formulas and solve problems together with the student.
  5. We practise similar tasks and focus on the parts that were difficult for the student.
  6. After the lesson, I record the completed lesson in my scheduling/accounting system and, when necessary, plan the next lesson.
